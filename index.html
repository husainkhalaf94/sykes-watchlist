How stocks are selected
  • Universe: NASDAQ, NYSE and AMEX listed stocks priced $1–$10 in pre-market, float under 20M shares.
  • Catalyst: a news headline within 36 hours for gappers, within 5 days for runners and dip-buys.
  • Relative volume ≥ 5x: pre-market volume against a normal pre-market (10% of the 20-day average daily volume), or yesterday's volume against its 20-day average, whichever is higher.
  • Supernova gapper: up 20%+ pre-market. Plan: buy the break of the pre-market high, stop near pre-market VWAP.
  • Morning-panic dip-buy: ran 50%+ within the last 1–5 sessions and is now 10%+ off its peak. Plan: wait for the early flush, buy the first strong bounce.
  • Breakout / multi-day runner: closed at a 20-day high or 2+ green days in a row on 3x+ volume, and holding up pre-market. Plan: buy the break of yesterday's high.
  • Red flags (shelf or offering filings in the last 120 days, reverse splits, foreign IPOs under a year old, SPACs, warrants) are shown as amber badges and cost points in the ranking. They are not excluded.
  • Top 10 by score: gap size, relative volume, smaller float, fresher news, number of setups, minus red flags.

Data from Yahoo Finance, Finviz, Nasdaq and SEC EDGAR via a scheduled GitHub Actions scan. Pre-market data can be delayed or incomplete. Levels and plans are mechanical starting points, not advice: confirm on your own chart before you trade.
